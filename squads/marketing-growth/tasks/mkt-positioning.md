# mkt-positioning

## Task Definition

- **Responsible**: `brand-strategist`
- **Goal**: Define the "New Game" and our unique wedge in the market.

## Execution Workflow

1. **Baseline**: Read `CONTEXT.md` and the latest `mkt-competitor-map`.
2. **Category Design**: Apply the **POV Framework**.
   - What is the "Old Game" everyone else is playing?
   - What is the "Change" in the world that makes the Old Game obsolete?
   - What is the "New Category" we are building?
3. **Wedge Drafting**: Write `docs/marketing/OUTPUTS/brand/positioning.md`.
   - **Category Name**: 2-3 words.
   - **The Wedge**: Why are we 10x better for a specific sub-segment?
   - **Strategic Narrative**: The story of the "Shift".
4. **Approval**: **Elicit feedback** on the category name and narrative.

## Post-Conditions

- [ ] Positioning document is saved.
- [ ] `docs/marketing/MESSAGING.md` is updated with the core category narrative.

## Verification

- Run `*mkt-claims-check` to ensure the "Differentiators" are grounded.
