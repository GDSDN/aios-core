# Claims check checklist

Use this checklist before shipping any external-facing copy (landing pages, ads, emails, listings).

- [ ] Every quantitative claim has a citation.
- [ ] Every qualitative claim is either:
  - supported by evidence, or
  - labeled as hypothesis/assumption.
- [ ] No implied endorsement ("trusted by", "used by") unless backed by proof.
- [ ] No competitor comparisons without named sources and date.
- [ ] No legal/medical/security promises unless validated with the owner.
- [ ] Pricing/offer terms match `docs/marketing/OFFERS.md`.
- [ ] CTA and funnel steps match the actual product.
